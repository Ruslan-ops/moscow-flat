## 2. Extra costs
- Commission: no legal cap; 2026 sources say typically 50-100% of monthly rent (comfort segment 50-70%, economy up to 80-100%); journalist test Nov 2026: from 50%, up to 100% [S] https://msk1.ru/text/realty/2026/11/07/76521112/ , https://andomian.ru/kto-platit-rieltoru-pri-arende-pravila-i-razmery-komissij-v-2026-godu , https://arenda.yandex.ru/journal/post/komissiya-pri-syome-kvartiry/
- Paid by tenant normally; after signing/move-in, not before viewing [S]. Direct from owner = no commission.
- Deposit: usually 1 month (sometimes 0.5-1; can be split into 2-3 months); goes to owner, refundable; commission is non-refundable [S] snippets.
- Utilities (1-room) [G]: ~5-9k RUB/month (heating, water, electricity, maintenance), by meters; paid by tenant on top of rent; check whether internet/intercom/капремонт are included. Record meter readings at handover.
- Typical move-in: first month + deposit + commission = ~2.5-3x rent.

## 3. Renting for 1-3 months
- Not a mainstream product; standard is 11-month contract [G]. Demand exists (Profi.ru requests "2-3 months") [S].
- Yandex Realty has listings from 1 month; Cian some from 3 months; owner listings often 2+ months; temporary registration often not provided on short terms [S snippets].
- Avito/Cian "на несколько месяцев" filters [G, not verified]; Cian "studio per month" page (many are daily): https://www.cian.ru/snyat-kvartiru-studiu-na-mesyac/

- Aparthotels per month [S] https://101hotels.com/main/cities/moskva/apartments_month : ~142k (Happy Home, Leninsky), 168k (Sretenka), 282k (Intermark). YE'S aparthotel studios are listed on Cian (Mitino, Shelepikha, Penyagino), no prices obtained. Station/Well/Like/Vertical/coliving prices: not found.
- Premium vs 11-month: no data; [G] expect +20-50% for 1-3 months, aparthotels ~2x.
- Tips [G]: written contract even for 1-2 months; verify owner via EGRN; deposit return terms in writing; avoid lump-sum prepayment, pay monthly.

## 4. Red flags / checks
Sources: https://mperspektiva.ru/topics/gid-arendatora-2026-kak-snyat-kvartiru-i-ne-stat-zhertvoy-moshennikov/ , https://www.cian.ru/stati-fejkovaja-arenda-kak-raspoznat-moshennichestvo-311656/ , https://realty.yandex.ru/journal/post/komissiya-rieltoru-skolko-platit/ , https://www.mentoday.ru/life/news/18-05-2025/kak-raspoznat-moshennikov-pri-arende-jilya-oni-vydayut-sebya-za-hozyaev/
- Prepayment/deposit before viewing = scam; real agents take commission after contract signing.
- Price well below market, stock photos, "other tenants, pay now" pressure.
- Request to transfer deposit/rent to third-party card ("wife/brother"); pay only owner named in passport/EGRN.
- "Owner" who is actually an agent (hidden commission, no documents); fake "client" schemes targeting realtors.
- Verify [G for details]: passport vs EGRN extract (order it yourself), co-owners' consent / power of attorney, written contract with inventory act, meter readings, deposit terms.
- Viewing checklist [G]: visit evening/weekend for noise; listen through walls/ceiling; mold in corners, window slopes, behind furniture; ceiling stains; water pressure/hot water, pipes, leaks under sink; trash chute smell; avoid 1st floor (noise, cold) and top floor (leaks); windows to road/tram/railway; window quality; radiators; wiring; elevator/intercom; mobile signal.
